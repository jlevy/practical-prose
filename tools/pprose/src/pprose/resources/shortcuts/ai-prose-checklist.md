# AI-Prose Checklist

The compact digest of `pprose guidelines ai-prose-corrections`, for
loading into a drafting or quick-review context.
Every rule here is expanded there with corrections, exceptions, and evidence; when a
rule seems wrong for the genre, check the full catalog’s carve-outs before overriding.

## While Writing

1. **Name the actor.** No inanimate things doing human actions (*the data tells us*), no
   passives that hide who acted.
   When no one specific fits, use *you*.
2. **State the point without announcing it.** No throat-clearing (*Here’s the thing:*,
   *The truth is*), no previews of the next sentence, no rhetorical setups (*What if I
   told you…*).
3. **Be specific or be silent.** No vague declaratives (*the implications are
   significant*); name the implication.
   No *every*/*always*/*never* where a count or example should be.
4. **Earn rhetorical force.** No *“This isn’t X. This is Y.”* or *“Not X. Not Y. Z.”*
   unless X is a position a real reader holds.
   No fragment templates (*That’s it.
   That’s the [thing].*).
5. **Vary rhythm deliberately.** No metronomic sentence lengths, repeated punchy
   one-liner endings, or reflexive three-item lists.
6. **Trust the reader.** No softening, permission-granting (*and that’s okay*), or
   hand-holding. State facts; let readers conclude.
7. **End when the content ends.** No conclusions that restate (*In conclusion*,
   *Overall*), no empty closers (*only time will tell*), no chat artifacts (*I hope this
   helps*).

## Words and Phrases to Cut or Replace

- **Hollow verbs and nouns:** *delve*, *dive into*, *unpack*, *harness*, *leverage*,
  *utilize*, *foster*, *facilitate*, *enhance*, *elevate*, *landscape*, *realm*,
  *tapestry*, *solutions* → use the specific verb or domain name.
- **Filler adjectives:** *robust*, *seamless*, *streamlined*, *intuitive*, *holistic*,
  *comprehensive*, *straightforward*, *crucial*, *pivotal*, *meticulous*, *nuanced* →
  give the measurement or named property, or cut.
- **Marketing register:** *game-changer*, *cutting-edge*, *supercharge*, *unlock*,
  *future-proof*, *10x*, *next-level*, *in the age of AI* → quantify or cut.
- **Inflated significance:** *a testament to*, *plays a pivotal role in shaping*,
  *underscores the importance of*, *cannot be overstated*, trailing *…, highlighting X*
  → state what depends on it, or cut.
- **Copula avoidance:** *serves as*, *stands as*, *boasts*, *features* → write *is* and
  *has*.
- **Vague attribution:** *experts say*, *studies show*, *industry reports suggest* →
  name the source or own the judgment.
- **Mechanical transitions:** *Furthermore*, *Additionally*, *Moreover*, *Moving
  forward*, *At the end of the day*, *In other words*, *It’s worth noting that*, *That
  said* → name the actual relationship or drop the connector.
- **Boilerplate frames (always cut):** *In today’s fast-paced world*, *When it comes
  to*, *Whether you’re X or Y*, *In conclusion* plus restatement, *The future looks
  bright*, *Key Takeaways* that parrot the body.
- **Engagement bait (always cut):** *Let that sink in*, *Read that again*, *This changes
  everything*, *What nobody tells you*, *Most people don’t realize*.
- **Chat artifacts (always cut):** *Certainly!*, *Great question*, *I hope this helps*,
  *As an AI language model*, *[insert X]* placeholders, stray citation tokens.

## Density Flags

Legitimate in moderation; AI register at high density: intensifier adverbs (*really*,
*just*, *actually*, *genuinely*), Wh- sentence openers, three-item lists, em dashes,
punchy paragraph endings, questions answered immediately, hedge stacking (*may*,
*might*, *potentially*), bullet/bold/emoji density, rotating synonyms for one referent.
Inspect; keep what carries information.

## House Punctuation

Em dashes only when they are the best punctuation, never spaced (*context—like this*).
Follow CMOS hyphenation: no *-ly* adverb hyphens; hyphenate compound modifiers before a
noun, not after.
